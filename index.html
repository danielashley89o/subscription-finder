How do I get a CSV file from my bank?
  1. Log in to your bank's website or app.
  2. Look for Statements, Transactions or Export.
  3. Choose CSV (sometimes called "spreadsheet" or "Excel") and pick the last 12 months.

Twelve months works best. Yearly renewals only show up if the statement covers them.
