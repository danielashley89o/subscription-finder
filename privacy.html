Subscription Finder

Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Short version: your bank statements never leave your device, we don't use analytics or cookies, and the only personal details we receive are the ones you give the payment provider when you buy.

Your bank statements

Subscription Finder reads your statement inside your web browser. The statement is never uploaded, stored on a server or shared with anyone. The page that reads statements is set up so that it can't connect to the internet at all, and it doesn't keep the statement after you close the page.

What's saved on your device

To save you redoing things, your browser remembers your choices (Keep, Cancel it, Not a subscription), any free trials you add, and your licence key. These stay in your browser on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

Buying a licence

Purchases are handled by Stripe, which sells the licence to you as our reseller ("merchant of record"). Stripe collects your name, email address, country and payment details to process the payment, send your receipt and handle tax, under its own privacy policy. We can see your name, email address and order details in our Stripe account, so we can help with support and refunds and keep the records the law requires.

Unlocking

Straight after you buy, the unlock page sends your payment reference to our server, which asks Stripe whether the payment went through and then gives you your licence key. When you type in a licence key, the unlock page sends that key to our server to check it. Nothing else is sent, and we don't keep a record of these checks.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the service. We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you (your order details). Email danielashley890@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
