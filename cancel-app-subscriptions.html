How to cancel a subscription on iPhone or Android

Deleting an app doesn't cancel its subscription. If you subscribed through the app, the payment is taken by Apple or Google, and you cancel it there.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap the subscription you want to stop, then Cancel Subscription.

You can also get there from the App Store app: tap your profile picture at the top, then Subscriptions.

If a subscription isn't in the list, it isn't billed through Apple. It may be billed under a family member's Apple account, or directly by the company (see below).

On an Android phone

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Tap the subscription, then Cancel subscription, and follow the steps.

If you have more than one Google account on the phone, switch accounts from the profile picture and check each one.

When it isn't in either list

If you signed up on a company's website rather than in an app, the subscription is billed directly to your card or PayPal. Cancel it on the company's website, usually under Account, Billing or Membership. For PayPal, look under Automatic payments in PayPal's settings too.

What happens after you cancel

  • You normally keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.
  • Check your next bank statement to make sure the charge has stopped.
  • If a company keeps charging your card after you've cancelled, your bank or card provider can stop the payments.
